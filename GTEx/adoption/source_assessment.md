# HZ2 source assessment

HZ2 uses GTEx V8 RNASeQCv1.1.9 TPM, sample attributes, and subject
phenotypes from the GTEx Portal. The original DRC ETL and its exact
sample-signature release are unavailable. HZ2 is consequently a documented
scientific reimplementation: it must be reported as scientifically comparable,
not set-equivalent, after a full V8 run and review.
