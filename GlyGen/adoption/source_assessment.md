# GlyGen source assessment

Both models are scientific reimplementations. The historical glycosylated-protein GMT is reconstructed from the same-release v1.12.1 citation and masterlist tables without using its legacy mapping file. The enzyme model transforms cached live GlyGen glycan-detail/Sandbox responses, whose content can drift from the September 2026 validation snapshot.

Legacy GMTs are validation-only. A full validation must report shared-term and global term-gene metrics and preserve, rather than suppress, candidate-only or target-only terms.
